Privacy policy

Let the Record Show. Last updated 9 November 2026.

Let the Record Show publishes short history videos on Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and X. We use each platform's official tools to publish our own posts, read the statistics for our own posts, and hide comments that break our rules.

What we collect

We don't run accounts for you, sell anything or collect personal data on this site. When you comment on one of our posts, the platform shows us your public name and the comment, as it shows them to everyone. We keep a log of comments we hide (the text, the public name and the reason) so that mistakes can be put right. Post statistics we read are totals, not information about individual people.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share any of this with anyone, don't use it for advertising, and don't combine it with other data.

How long we keep it

The hidden-comment log is kept for up to 12 months.

Data deletion

To have anything about you removed from our records, send a message to the Let the Record Show Page on Facebook or Instagram saying which comment or account it concerns. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm. Deleting a comment from the platform itself is done through that platform.

Changes

Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new date.
